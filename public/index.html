Version 0.4.0-beta.1 · Apple Silicon Macs · macOS 14 or newer

Everything you check before enrolling, in one window.

Search a course once and BYE gathers what you'd normally collect across a dozen tabs. It never ranks a “best” professor; you compare and decide.

01

Course search

Type a code like math 5a or stat c1000. BYE opens on an overview of the class (sections, professors, open seats, formats and terms) before you pick anyone.

02

Class details and seats

Each section shows its format (in person, hybrid, online live or async), term, campus, units, and open seats with the waitlist, straight from PCC's schedule.

Plenty of seats 10 or fewer 5 or fewer
03

Days that fit you

Tap the days you can make it to campus and BYE shows classes that meet on every day you picked. Turn on Fully online only to skip campus entirely.

04

Ratings and student reviews

Overall rating, difficulty and “would take again” side by side, plus original reviews for your course or the professor's others, each linked to its source.

Install in a minute.

  1. Download the AppleSilicon.dmg file.
  2. Open the downloaded DMG.
  3. Drag BYE Beta into your Applications folder.
  4. Eject the DMG and open BYE Beta from Applications.
Seeing a security warning?

BYE is signed but not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS may block the first launch. If you trust the download, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

Updates install themselves.

BYE checks for a new version about once a day and installs it when you quit. Keep it in Applications so it can update. You can turn this off in Settings.
