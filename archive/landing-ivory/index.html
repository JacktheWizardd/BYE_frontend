How it works

Three steps, one tab.

  1. Type a class

    A code like MATH 5A or a title, plus your semester and the days you want class.

  2. Compare professors

    Rating, difficulty and “would take again” for everyone teaching it, sorted your way.

  3. Read and decide

    Student reviews for your class, with links back to the source. BYE never picks for you.

What you see

Everything you check before enrolling, in one place.

01

Sections and times

Days, times, format and open seats for every section of the class.

02

Ratings side by side

Overall rating, difficulty, number of ratings and “would take again” for each professor.

03

Student reviews

Reviews for the course you're taking, or from the professor's other courses, with links to the original source.

04

Days that fit you

Pick your class days and BYE shows the sections that meet on them. Online classes always show.

On a Mac?

BYE is also a Mac app.

The same search in a native window, plus review summaries made on your Mac with Apple's on-device AI. Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or newer.

Download for Mac

  1. Download the AppleSilicon.dmg file.
  2. Open it and drag BYE Beta into Applications.
  3. Eject the DMG and open BYE Beta from Applications.
Seeing a security warning?

BYE is signed but not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS may block the first launch. If you trust the download, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

Updates install themselves.

BYE checks for a new version about once a day and installs it when you quit. Keep it in Applications so it can update.

No sign-in or API key. Internet is needed for class schedules and professor data. Windows isn't planned right now.

You're an early tester.

Found a bug, a wrong professor match, or have an idea? Tell me. BYE is built by a PCC student, and tester feedback decides what gets fixed first.
